# Alder Ripple Koi: design notes

**Fantasy:** tending a koi pond with nothing but patience.

**Control:** tap anywhere on the water. A ring expands at 170 units/s up to 210 units. When the ring front reaches a koi it gives a push away from the drop, which is stronger the closer the drop was. Water drag slowly calms every fish.

**Rules:** a koi rests when it glides into an ensō of its own colour (one fish per ring). Stones deflect koi, koi nudge each other, and currents carry them. There is no failing: you can always tap, undo or start again.

**Seals:** within par = 3, par + 2 = 2, otherwise 1. Par is the solver's drop count, and the solver only drops a ripple once the water is calm, so par is always reachable by hand.

**Chapters**
1. Garden Pond: one or two koi, stones as rails.
2. Temple Stream: currents to ride and resist.
3. Moon Pool: kohaku, ogon and sumi koi together, tight gates, night palette.

**Look:** rice paper UI, vermilion hanko seals, kanji level numbers, and the pond as a framed painting. Music and effects are tuned so a run of taps sounds like a koto phrase.
